You can get a long way with AI before spending anything. These tools all offer a free tier that is useful rather than a teaser. Free plans change often, so treat the limits below as a guide and check the vendor's site.

## For everyday questions and drafting

[ChatGPT](/tools/chatgpt/), [Claude](/tools/claude/) and [Gemini](/tools/gemini/) all have free tiers. The free versions limit how much you can use the most capable models, but they handle email drafts, summaries and brainstorming well. Try the same task in all three; the differences in style are easier to feel than to describe.

## For research with sources

[Perplexity](/tools/perplexity/) answers questions using live web results and shows citations. It is the quickest way to get a sourced overview of an unfamiliar topic.

## For cleaner writing

[Grammarly](/tools/grammarly/) catches spelling and grammar mistakes in your browser and email for free. [QuillBot](/tools/quillbot/) rephrases sentences when you are stuck on wording.

## For images

[Leonardo.Ai](/tools/leonardo-ai/) gives daily free credits, and [Adobe Firefly](/tools/adobe-firefly/) includes a monthly allowance. If you have a capable graphics card and some patience, [Stable Diffusion](/tools/stable-diffusion/) runs on your own computer with no per-image cost.

## For coding

[GitHub Copilot](/tools/github-copilot/) has a free tier with a monthly allowance of completions and chat. [Cursor](/tools/cursor/) offers a free plan that lets you try its editor and agent.

## For voice and meetings

[ElevenLabs](/tools/elevenlabs/) includes a small monthly allowance of text-to-speech for non-commercial use. [Otter.ai](/tools/otter-ai/) transcribes a limited number of meeting minutes each month.

## What free plans usually leave out

- **Commercial rights.** Several free tiers, particularly for audio and images, do not license output for commercial use.
- **Privacy.** Free image tools often make your generations public.
- **Capacity.** Expect lower limits, slower responses at busy times, or older models.

When a free tool becomes part of your weekly routine, that is the signal to consider paying. Until then, use our [buying checklist](/blog/how-to-choose-an-ai-tool/) to compare options.
