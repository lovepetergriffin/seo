## The real difference

On most everyday tasks the two are closer than the marketing suggests. Ask either to summarise a report or draft an email and you will get a usable answer. The gap shows at the edges.

**ChatGPT is broader.** Image generation, voice conversations, custom GPTs and a large integration ecosystem all live in one subscription. If you want a single AI app for everything, this is the argument for it.

**Claude is deeper on text and code.** It tends to produce prose that needs less editing, holds on to detailed instructions through long conversations and is a favourite among developers for multi-step coding work.

## How to decide

1. List the three tasks you would use an assistant for most often.
2. Run each one in both free tiers with the same prompt.
3. Pay for the one whose output you edited least.

If image generation is on your list, that settles it in favour of ChatGPT. If your list is reports, analysis and code, give Claude the first try.
