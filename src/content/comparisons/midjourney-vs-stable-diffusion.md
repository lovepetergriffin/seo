## Two different philosophies

Midjourney is a finished product. You type a prompt, and it applies a strong aesthetic sense on your behalf. You trade control for consistency and speed.

Stable Diffusion is a set of open models and a community toolchain. You choose the model, the interface and the add-ons. Nothing is decided for you, which is both the appeal and the cost.

## When Midjourney wins

- You need a striking image in the next five minutes.
- You do not own a capable GPU and do not want to manage software.
- You value a consistent, polished look over exact composition.

## When Stable Diffusion wins

- You need to match a pose, layout or product shape exactly, using ControlNet.
- Your images must stay private or on your own infrastructure.
- You want to train a model on your own style, character or product.
- You generate at high volume and per-image pricing would add up.

Many professionals use both: Midjourney for exploring ideas, Stable Diffusion for the controlled final asset.
