Claude tends to win people over on tone. Drafts need less editing to stop sounding like AI, and it is unusually good at sticking to detailed instructions across a long conversation.

If your work is mostly text and code, it is a serious alternative to ChatGPT. If you need image generation in the same tool, it is not.
