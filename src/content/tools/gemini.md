Gemini makes the most sense when your documents, email and calendar are already with Google. Asking it to find a detail in an old email thread or summarise a Drive folder is where it pulls ahead of standalone assistants.

Outside the Google ecosystem the advantage shrinks, and the choice comes down to which assistant's style you prefer.
