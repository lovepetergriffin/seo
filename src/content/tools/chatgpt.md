ChatGPT is the safe default. If you are not sure which assistant to pay for, it does the widest range of things competently, and most tutorials you find online assume you are using it.

The reason to look elsewhere is usually specific: you want longer, more careful writing, tighter Google Workspace integration, or answers that always come with sources.
